📦 Bulk Add Questions
Paste questions in the format below. Each will be added to Firestore.
EXAM: KCSE
SUBJECT: Biology
TOPIC: Cell Structure
QUESTION: State three functions of the cell membrane.
MARKPOINT: Controls what enters and leaves the cell
MARKPOINT: Selectively permeable
MARKPOINT: Protects the cell contents
EXPLAIN: The cell membrane is the selectively permeable boundary of the cell.
---

EXAM: KCSE
SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Quadratic Equations
QUESTION: Solve x² − 5x + 6 = 0
MARKPOINT: x = 2
MARKPOINT: x = 3
EXPLAIN: Factorise as (x − 2)(x − 3) = 0.
---

Rules:
• Use --- between questions
• Every question needs EXAM, SUBJECT, TOPIC, QUESTION, and at least one MARKPOINT
• EXAM must be KCSE, KJSEA, or KPSEA
• Each MARKPOINT is one line
• EXPLAIN is optional
